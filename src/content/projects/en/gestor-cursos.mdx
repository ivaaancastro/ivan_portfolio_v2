A distributed application designed to handle records, queries and data administration in a client-server environment.

## Highlights

- Service exposed over **REST** and **Apache Thrift** to define services and communicate efficiently across languages.
- Modular and scalable approach.
- MySQL persistence with JDBC DAOs.
