A personal project focused on sports data analysis. The goal is to bring training metrics together in one place and use Artificial Intelligence to generate personalized plans.

#### Key features:

- **Strava integration:** OAuth connection to import and analyze activities automatically.
- **Smart Coach (AI):** a module that uses generative AI to interpret fitness level and suggest sessions.
- **Modern backend:** data persistence and authentication handled with **Supabase**.
- **Interactive dashboard:** charts of progress and training load distribution with Recharts.
